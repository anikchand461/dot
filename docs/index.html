Features

Everything you need. Nothing in your way.

A full-screen page, one floating tool dock, and the features that make handwritten notes useful.

✍️

Pressure-sensitive ink

Real stylus pressure on macOS and Windows. Fountain, ballpoint, brush and calligraphy pens, smoothed into curves while you write.

🔷

Diagrams, Excalidraw-style

11 shapes with fills, dashed and dotted lines, bendable arrows, and labels typed inside boxes. Draw a rough shape and hold to snap it clean.

📄

Annotate any PDF

Write on slides and papers. Exports keep the original pages, with selectable text, and draw your ink on top as vectors.

🔤

Handwriting fonts

Shantell Sans, Caveat, Kalam, Patrick Hand and Indie Flower, chosen from a live-preview dropdown and embedded in PDFs.

🖼️

Paste & crop

Press ⌘V to paste a screenshot, then resize and crop it on the page. Use any picture as your paper background.

🔒

Yours, offline

No account, no telemetry. Notebooks are plain files in a folder you choose, autosaved every 20 seconds.

Home

All your notebooks, at a glance.

dot! opens to a Home page with every notebook in your folder, each shown with a live preview of its first page.

  • Search, sort by recent or A–Z
  • Rename in place, move to Trash
  • Start new notebooks with your favourite paper
Pens & fonts

Pick a nib. Pick a voice.

Tune pressure sensitivity, stabiliser and tapered ends, then type in a font that still looks handwritten.

  • Four real pen types with nib previews
  • Colour wheel and your 10 recent colours
  • Plain, ruled, grid and dot paper in 15 colours
Why dot!

“I love Xournal++.
I just wanted it to feel modern.”

Xournal++ is a great open-source app: it handles a pen well, annotates PDFs, and keeps notes in open files. But day to day it felt dated, with a crowded toolbar, no filled shapes or bendable arrows, and no home for your notes.

dot! keeps what makes Xournal++ good and rebuilds the experience around a clean page and a single dock. It reads and writes .xopp, so you can switch back and forth: nothing is locked in.

dot! vs Xournal++

Lighter on your CPU. Faster to open.

Both apps ran the same automated workload on the same Apple Silicon Mac: a 58-page notebook with 17,464 strokes, with drawing, scrolling, zooming and erasing.
